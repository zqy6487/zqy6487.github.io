# Qiyun (Claire) Zheng

[Academic website](https://zqy6487.github.io/) · [Research](https://zqy6487.github.io/#research) · [Publications](https://zqy6487.github.io/#publications)

I attend Shanghai Foreign Language School Affiliated to SISU. My interests include robotics, autonomous navigation, and environmental monitoring.

My main project, **WetlandSentinel**, is an ellipsoidal amphibious robot for wetland ecological monitoring. Alongside prototyping and testing, I have explored the practical requirements of wetland monitoring through field visits in Shanghai and Beihai, Guangxi. Conversations with conservation practitioners are informing further work on deployment, maintenance, and observation with minimal disturbance to wildlife.

I also work on competition robotics and visual-inertial odometry with FRC Team 6487. My website includes further details on these projects, publications, awards, and activities.

**Paper:** Qiyun Zheng. *WetlandSentinel: An Ellipsoidal Amphibious Robot for Wetland Ecological Monitoring.* IEEE ICDM Workshops (ICDMW), Teen Research Track, 2026. [PDF](https://zqy6487.github.io/WetlandSentinel/WetlandSentinel_Qiyun_Zheng.pdf)
